# 08 — De-risking: The Cheap-Test Plan

> *Design the experiments, not the plant.* (Principle 4.)

For every capital-heavy commitment, the first move is the cheapest test that turns its
gating guesses into trusted numbers. Each pilot below lists the variable it collapses, a
rough cost/time, and the decision it gates. Most pilots **co-locate** to share
infrastructure.

## The income test (gates everything)

| Test | Collapses | Cost/time | Gates |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sign the first licensing deals** | True extraction rate from AI giants (the single biggest unknown) | Legal + sales effort | Phase-1 build scale; the whole glidepath (`02`) |
| IP/delivery-moat review | Is the toll "un-removable" (SaaS/server-side vs. patent-only)? | Low | Whether the income survives retaliation |

## The desal + greenhouse pilot (co-located; gates the $5.5B plant)

A **rented containerized RO skid** + a **Sundrop-style pilot greenhouse**, both on real
Sea-of-Cortez feedwater, sited together so the water test feeds the food test.

| Variable | Test | Collapses | Cost/time |
|---|---|---|---|
| True energy intensity (kWh/m³) | RO skid on actual feedwater | Solar requirement; whole energy budget | weeks, low-mid 5 figures |
| **Membrane durability under solar intermittency** | Run skid on a solar-following duty cycle | Whether expensive firming/storage is needed — the architecture fork | +1 season |
| Site solar output (CF, dust/heat derate) | NREL NSRDB data (free) + small reference array | Panels-per-m³ | weeks, near-free |
| Brine/mineral recovery yield + energy | Bench-process the skid's reject stream | Mg/mineral economics; ZLD feasibility (permitting) | lab bench, cheap |
| **Storage requirement** | *Model it* once the above are measured | Storage sizing (the real cost driver) | days, simulation |
| Crop yield per L / per m² / per kWh | Pilot greenhouse on candidate crops | CEA economics; what to grow vs. import | 1 season |
| Deuterium extraction energy | Bench, on the skid's stream | Heavy-water product economics | cheap |

**Dependency note:** storage sizing — the most expensive single decision — is computed
*last*, downstream of four cheap measurements. Guess it first and you over-buy (fails
Cheap) or under-build and cycle the membranes to death (fails Durable).

## The protein + garden pilot (gates the food/animal layer)

| Test | Collapses | Cost/time |
|---|---|---|
| Closed-loop protein cell: aquaponics tank + poultry flock + black-soldier-fly bin on the greenhouse's crop/food waste | Protein out per liter of water / per kg imported feed / per m² | a season, low 6 figures |
| Desert-garden demo plot (Sonoran crops, drip + greywater) | Real household water draw and yield; the gardens' true cost | a season, cheap |

## The aircrete pilot (gates the building system)

| Test | Collapses | Cost/time |
|---|---|---|
| Batch trials across the permeability/insulation/strength dial + additive mixes | Real strength/permeability/R per mix on local cement; shrinkage-crack behavior; QC reproducibility at scale | weeks, cheap |
| Evaporative-cooling-wall mock-up in desert heat | °F drop, water draw, comfort-humidity effect | a season, cheap |

## The governance pilot (gates the handoff)

| Test | Collapses | Cost/time |
|---|---|---|
| Draft + legally vet the constitution, CLT, co-op charters, and **transfer triggers** before any build | Whether the founder's exit is *structurally binding* (not discretionary) | legal effort |
| Seed one real business and run a trial transfer to a small worker co-op | Whether the sell-to-the-union mechanism actually works | months |

## Why this is the speed multiplier

~a few million dollars and roughly one season of co-located pilots **de-risks a multi-
billion-dollar build.** Every gating variable becomes trusted, so the subsequent build is
fast *and* confident. Rigor early → speed late.

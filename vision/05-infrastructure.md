# 05 — Infrastructure: Water, Energy, Food, Minerals

Four coupled systems. The binding constraint across all of them is **water and the energy
to move/clean it.**

## Water — desalination

- **Site reality:** Arizona is landlocked. The nearest saltwater is the **Sea of Cortez at
  Puerto Peñasco, ~60 mi south in Mexico.** A real precedent exists: Arizona advanced a
  **~$5.5B** desal + 200-mi pipeline-to-Phoenix proposal (IDE Technologies), 300k AF/yr
  scaling to 1M AF.
- **Economics:** ~**$2,500–3,500/acre-foot** (vs ~$1,000 for current CAP water);
  **~3–4 kWh/m³** SWRO with energy recovery.
- **Strategic fork (see `10`):** city-in-AZ with piped coastal plant (keeps the Arizona
  mission; adds binational dependency — the real stall risk: cross-border agreement, ESA,
  the totoaba/vaquita brine objection) **vs.** city-at-the-coast (cheaper water; breaks the
  Arizona-citizens mission). **Lean: city in AZ, plant coastal.**
- **Power interface: source-agnostic** (see Energy). Build the interface, not the
  dependency.

## Energy

- **Baseline (proven, durable): solar + firming powers desal**, and carries the green-end-
  state promise on its own, today. Solar is cheap (~$1.40–2.00/W commercial).
- **Energy budget is the real bottleneck** — solar is fought over by **desal + magnesium
  electrolysis + the city/factories**. This contention, not mineral concentration, is the
  constraint to model.
- **Fusion (Helion) = a green-end-state *option*, never a dependency.** Status (Feb 2026):
  Polaris running but **net electricity not demonstrated**; experts skeptical of the 2028
  Microsoft-PPA timeline. Durable-first + "never put unproven tech on the critical path of
  life-support infrastructure" rules it out as primary power. Posture:
  - **Solar baseline carries the water.**
  - **Source-agnostic power interface** so a 50 MWe fusion module can plug in later without
    redesign.
  - **A delivery-contingent PPA**, not equity — technology risk stays on Helion.
  - Revisit when Polaris shows net electricity. Track the Microsoft 2028 PPA as a free
    canary.

## Food — controlled-environment agriculture (mandatory)

**The water math forces this.** Arizona agriculture is **72–76% of all state water use**;
alfalfa needs **5–7 acre-feet/acre/yr** → at desal prices, ~$12,500–17,500/acre in water
alone. Conventional field farming on desalinated water is **impossible**.

- **Forced move (and proven): seawater greenhouses / CEA.** Precedent: **Sundrop Farms**
  (Port Augusta, AU) — 20-ha seawater greenhouse, solar-thermal desal, **16,000+ t
  tomatoes/yr (~15% of Australia's supply)**, no soil/groundwater/fossil/pesticide.
- **Water cut: 90–95%.** Tomatoes 60 L/kg (field) → **4 L/kg** (hi-tech recirculating
  greenhouse); lettuce 250 → 20 L/kg. This is what keeps farm water small enough to make
  the desal-fed settlement feasible.
- **Priority ordering dictates CEA:** open-field fails Durable (groundwater/soil mining)
  *and* Cheap (water-suicidal).
- **Closed loops:** seawater evaporative cooling (the permeable aircrete) = greenhouse
  climate control; brine-recovered minerals (K, Mg, Ca) = hydroponic nutrients; town
  wastewater = irrigation makeup; aquaponics (fish) = water-efficient protein; farm-to-
  commissary = food vertical integration.
- **Honest limit:** you cannot economically grow bulk staples (grains) even in CEA. **Grow
  fresh/perishable/high-value locally; import bulk calories.** Target food *resilience +
  fresh local supply*, not autarky. Minimize livestock (the alfalfa-for-cattle water sin).

### Gardens (soft-layer supplement)

Household gardens of easy-to-grow food are a **soft-layer wellbeing feature** (personal
control → wellbeing, place attachment; see `01`/`09`) — **not** a water-efficient calorie
source. Budget lean: drip + greywater, **desert-adapted Sonoran crops** (tepary beans,
nopal, chiles, squash, mesquite, amaranth). Value = agency, not tonnage.

### Animal protein — the Blackbird co-op model, desert-adapted

Adopt Blackbird Coop's **cooperative ownership structure wholesale** (federated small
producers → commonly-held processor + distributor → community control; maps onto your
vertices/edges/CLT). **Adapt the biology to the desert:**
- **Fish (aquaculture/aquaponics) is the protein backbone** — most water-efficient, already
  in the CEA system.
- **Poultry second** (efficient — chickens ~2:1 feed conversion vs. beef ~6–10:1; resolves
  the earlier "minimize livestock," which targeted *cattle*, not poultry). Close the feed
  loop: food/crop waste → **black soldier fly larvae** → poultry/fish feed; import grain
  for the balance.
- **Ruminants out** (or tightly limited with honest water accounting). Blackbird is
  humid-Virginia pasture-based — take the org model, not the pasture.

## Minerals — brine processing (cost-offset + permitting, not a fortune)

"Brine as the main product, water as byproduct" does **not** escape physics: seawater is
dilute in valuable things, concentrated in cheap ones — *only high-abundance species have
ever paid.* Maximizing mineral pull at plant scale **crashes the commodity markets** (one
300k-AF plant ≈ ~25% of US salt demand, ~half of global Mg).

What actually works:
- **Magnesium is the one real winner** — 63% of US Mg historically came from seawater/brine
  (Dow's Freeport plant ran 57 years). It died post-WWII from **expensive power** — which
  your captive solar fixes. Mg metal feeds the industrial base (alloys, structures).
- **Consume captively, don't sell** — Mg → alloys; salt → chlor-alkali (the Mg process
  co-produces chlorine); lithium → battery storage; gypsum/lime → concrete. This sidesteps
  the market-crash problem and delivers **supply-chain security** (the real prize).
- **Zero-liquid-discharge is permitting gold** — consuming the brine flips the worst
  environmental liability (the brine-kill objection) into a selling point.
- **Deuterium / heavy water** — the desal stream (155.76 ppm D₂O; ~58,000 t D₂O-equivalent
  throughput/yr at 300k AF) is a natural deuterium source → fusion fuel (trivial qty) +
  sellable heavy water (~$600/kg). **Direction is desal→fusion, not fusion→heavy water.**
  Cheap heavy water needs cheap power (solar bootstraps it). **Export-control flag:** heavy
  water is a nuclear-controlled material — licensed/tracked, not an open commodity.
- **Retire "mine precious minerals for profit"** — seawater is a terrible ore for anything
  scarce.

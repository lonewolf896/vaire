# 01 — Objective Function: Flourishing → Faster Science

The keel. Everything else is instrumental to this.

## The thesis

Happy, well-fed, secure, *unafraid* humans — given freedom — produce better, more
well-thought-out ideas than humans under stress, scarcity, and fear. The whole system is
a population-scale machine for **using human flourishing to do science faster.**

## Why it is true (three converging, independently-grounded mechanisms)

| Mechanism | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Scarcity is a cognitive tax** | Financial fear imposes a "bandwidth tax" — effective cognition drops **~13–14 IQ points** (gap between "superior" and "average"); even *anticipation* of strain impairs it. | Mullainathan & Shafir |
| **Positive affect broadens the mind** | Happiness widens attention, cognitive flexibility, creativity (broaden-and-build). Tiny mood lifts improve divergent thinking, via prefrontal dopamine. | Fredrickson; Isen |
| **Safety + autonomy → innovation** | Psychological safety is the **#1 predictor** of high-performing teams; fearless, autonomous teams generate more creative ideas and take more risks. | Edmondson; Google Project Aristotle |

These map onto the system's design: **remove the tax** (security), **broaden the search**
(positive affect), **free the voice** (autonomy + safety).

## The whole machine, re-read as a cognition engine

Every subsystem is a remover of a cognition-killer or a supplier of a booster:

- **At-cost commissary + good wages + real pensions** → reclaims the ~13-IQ-point scarcity
  tax. The grocery was never about food; it is about liberating bandwidth.
- **CLT housing you cannot be evicted from + own your own infill** → removes precarity,
  grants control.
- **Locally-soft personalization + home gardens** → autonomy and positive affect (the
  place-attachment evidence in `09`).
- **Union ownership + push-back-from-the-bottom** → Edmondson's *fearless organization*
  at civilizational scale; no one can be dominated or silenced.
- **Walkable, coherent-variety hexes** → the built environment as a mood input (the facade
  research in `09`).

## The rigor — where the naive version is wrong

1. **Not "remove all stress" — remove *fear/scarcity/powerlessness* while *preserving*
   challenge and purpose.** Performance follows an inverted-U with arousal (Yerkes-Dodson);
   flow requires challenge matched to skill. Comfort without purpose breeds complacency,
   not science. Target: *security + autonomy + hard problems + meaning.*
2. **Flourishing is necessary, not sufficient.** You still need the engine: instruments, a
   knowledge commons, collaboration density (agglomeration compounds it — see `03`),
   freedom to fail, and time.
3. **Especially potent for creative/divergent work** — exactly the cognitive mode the
   project targets. Do not oversell as a universal productivity boost.

## The Goodhart trap (the load-bearing constraint)

You **cannot demand the science** without re-introducing the fear that destroys it. The
moment flourishing becomes a quota — "we made you happy, now produce" — performance-fear
returns and the bandwidth tax roars back.

Therefore:
- Happiness must be treated as a **genuine end**; the science is an **emergent byproduct,
  a gift, not a tax.**
- Real freedom must include the **freedom to not produce.**
- **Non-coercion is structurally required by the objective function itself.** A system that
  extracts ideas cannot generate them. This is the same constraint as the founder's-paradox
  and the equilibrium design (`06`): the system must be free of whoever funds it, whoever
  founds it, and whoever governs it.

## The reframed ROI

The return on the funding is **not** GDP or even measured happiness. It is **cognitive
surplus converted to discovery**, with happiness as the mechanism and the endowment (`02`)
making it a *permanent* innovation engine.

Every design choice gets one test against this principle:

> *Does this remove a cognition-killer (scarcity / fear / powerlessness) or add a booster
> (security / positive affect / autonomy) — without re-introducing coercion?*

## Caveat on measurement

Do **not** build tight "happiness KPIs" tied to output — that is the Goodhart trap wearing
a dashboard. Measure inputs (is scarcity removed? is housing secure? is the union real?)
and let discovery emerge unmeasured-for. Sources: see `09`.

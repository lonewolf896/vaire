# Project Honeycomb — Master Plan

> **Working codename.** "Honeycomb" is a placeholder for the hex-lattice geometry; the
> project is unnamed. See `10-open-decisions-and-risks.md`.

## What this is

A plan to convert a high-value AI-efficiency invention — licensed to AI companies for
roughly 20% of their prior-year AI spend (US-only) — into a **permanent, self-sustaining
engine for human flourishing**, built as a federation of small, walkable, desert
hex-cities in/near Arizona, funded by an endowment, and governed so it can resist
capture by its own founder, its own monopolies, and its own success.

The deepest purpose is not the city. The city is **instrumental**. The objective is to
remove the cognitive taxes of scarcity, fear, and powerlessness from every mind in the
system, supply the boosters of security, positive affect, and autonomy, and let the
resulting flourishing produce **faster, better science** — as an emergent gift, never a
demanded quota. See `01-objective-function.md`.

## The one-paragraph mechanism

A market-native toll (IP licensing, enforced by the AI industry's own competitive greed)
funds deliberately **non-market** institutions (a community land trust, worker
cooperatives, a perpetual endowment). The income is a **bootstrap, not a foundation** —
it is raced into a permanent endowment before the payers find the exit, so the system
becomes independent of the leeched, just as it is designed to become independent of the
founder.

## Status & how to read this

This is a **living plan in bootstrap phase**. Nothing here is committed past the next
secured platform. Figures are grounded where possible (see `09`) but many are
**illustrative pending the cheap tests in `08`** — they are placeholders to be replaced
by measurement, not commitments. Treat every number as a hypothesis with a test attached.
